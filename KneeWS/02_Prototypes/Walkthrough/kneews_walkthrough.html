Step 6 · what the clinician gets

A draft that refuses to hide its gaps

The system writes only what it actually saw. Where it abstained, it leaves a hole and blocks the signature until a radiologist fills it — the opposite of a tool that quietly guesses.

KneeWS is a research and decision-support system. It is not a clinically validated diagnostic tool, it gives no diagnosis or treatment advice, and its referral guidance names a specialist category only — never a named doctor.
The point

Twelve probabilities are the research output. The gap that blocks a signature is the product decision — a model that knows what it does not know is safer than one that is always confident.

Demonstration data. Every scan, report, name and number on these screens is invented to show the workflow — none of it is a real patient or a measured result.
