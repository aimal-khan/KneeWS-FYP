Research Pipeline · Offline Training Weak Supervision
  1. F3 Read raw multilingual hospital reports across diverse sites (Urdu, German, Spanish, English)
  2. F4 Sort each of the 12 findings into present absent uncertain unmentioned
  3. F5 Calculate empirical state reliability against expert referee cohort → continuous probability labels
  4. F6 Train multi-series 3D CNN on probability targets with variance regularisation
  5. F10 Benchmark against hard 1/0 pseudo-labelling and evaluate calibration on held-out referee cohort
Clinical Workstation · Inference Zero-Report Inference
  1. F1 Ingest study archive (DICOM series folder, NIfTI volumes, or zipped study)
  2. F2 Standardise series, reconstruct planes (Sagittal, Coronal, Axial), and normalise intensities
  3. F6 Incur twelve abnormality probabilities with 95% Bayesian credible intervals — no report needed
  4. F8 Interactive PACS slice viewer with localized attention heatmaps
  5. F7 Grounded draft report with mandatory gap lock · F9 Specialist care pathway routing
