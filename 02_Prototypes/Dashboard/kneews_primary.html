KneeWS Diagnostic Suite & Research Map

One unified system, two decoupled paths. Multilingual radiology reports train the AI without gold labels; images alone drive inference at test time.

Training path · research loop Offline ML
  1. F3 Ingest raw multilingual reports across clinical sites (Urdu, German, Spanish, English)
  2. F4 Sort each of the 12 findings into present absent uncertain unmentioned
  3. F5 Measure empirical state reliability against expert-checked subset → calibrated probability labels
  4. F6 Train multi-series deep knee MRI network directly on probability distributions
  5. F10 Benchmark against hard 1/0 pseudo-labelling and evaluate calibration on held-out referee cohort
Deployment path · clinical product Zero-Report Inference
  1. F1 Receive multi-format study (DICOM series folder, NIfTI volumes, or packaged archive)
  2. F2 Axial, sagittal & coronal reconstruction, slice standardisation, and scanner normalization
  3. F6 Multi-label abnormality analysis predicting 12 probabilities with Bayesian credible bounds
  4. F8 Interactive PACS evidence review — inspect localized heatmaps on corresponding slices
  5. F7 Grounded draft report with sign-off lock · F9 Specialist referral triage guidance
